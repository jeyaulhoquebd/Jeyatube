Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to **JTube**. We are dedicated to providing a clean, educational, and inspiring video platform focused on Islam, Science, AI, and Machine Learning. This Privacy Policy clarifies how interactions, custom profiles, and actions are handled on our platform.

2. Information Processing & Interaction

JTube operates an **Admin-Only upload model**, meaning standard users cannot publish videos. However, when you engage with content, the platform registers the following interactions:

  • Comments: The screen name and comment text you input into public interaction forms.
  • Engagement Metrics: Video likes, counts, and view parameters.
  • Subscriptions: Channels you choose to follow, which populate your navigation feed sidebar.

3. Local Storage Framework (LocalStorage)

Data Transparency: JTube does not use remote relational cloud server databases. All channel data updates, interaction tallies, and commentary structures run entirely within your device's web browser using Web Storage API (LocalStorage).

Because your data remains localized inside your browser:

  • We do not collect, view, sell, or rent your private information to third-party advertisers.
  • Clearing your web browser cache or clearing local site storage will completely reset all configurations, custom comments, and channel subscriptions back to factory defaults.

4. Admin Content Moderation

Only the platform site Administrator has the structural authority to publish or purge media items inside the localized layout feed index. Regular site users possess zero backend upload access privileges.

5. Managing Your Data

You possess complete administrative leverage over your personal data footprint. If you wish to delete your system data record metrics, simply open your preferred browser parameters and clear your **Site Cookies and Local Storage Data**.
