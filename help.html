Help & Support

Find answers about navigating the JTube ecosystem.

How do I upload videos on JTube?

Standard users cannot upload video files. To preserve high-quality content focused strictly on Islam, Science, AI, and Machine Learning, **only the platform administrator can post videos** through the Admin Dashboard.

Why did my comments and subscriptions disappear?

JTube utilizes a localized browser configuration storage engine (LocalStorage). If you clear your browser cookies, system cache, or site preferences, your saved comments and subscription lists will be completely reset.

How do I contact the Admin?

You can head over to our Feedback Page to draft suggestions. It will compile your report and route it directly to the designated support desk mailbox.
